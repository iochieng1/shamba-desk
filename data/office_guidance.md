# Office guidance (short rains, LM1 zone, Kanduyi)

Editable by the office. The agent reads this file on every run. These are
general extension rules of thumb, NOT yet validated by a Kanduyi officer;
see README "What is unfinished".

- Planting: advise planting only once rains are established, roughly 20 mm or
  more over the past 7 days with more rain forecast. Dry planting risks seed loss.
- If the next 7 days look dry (under ~10 mm forecast), advise waiting and
  keeping seed dry rather than planting.
- Maize top-dressing (CAN or urea) at knee height, about 6 weeks after
  planting, only when the soil is moist.
- Fall armyworm in maize: scout whorls on 20 plants per field twice a week.
  Act when fresh damage is on more than about 1 in 5 plants at early whorl.
  Product choice is the officer's: say "contact the office for an approved
  product", never name a product or dose.
- Bean stem maggot: confirm on the ground before any treatment advice.
- Beans in store: when the nearest market price rises sharply (over ~20% in a
  month), selling part of the stock now can make sense; keep the rest dry.
- Heavy rain forecast (over ~40 mm in a day): advise clearing drainage
  furrows and delaying fertiliser so it is not washed away.
